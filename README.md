## 📋 Overview - Holistic Solutions

### 1. Core Mission & Vision
The **Nexus Anima** project creates an innovative, 5-hectare retreat and recovery campus integrating traditional spiritual wisdom (Zen, Tibetan, Zoroastrian, Islamic, Christian, Torah, Shamanic traditions) with modern psychological science and Web3 asset tokenization ($NEXUS).

### 2. Operational Roadmap Summary (18-Month Plan)
1. **Months 1–2:** Land Acquisition & Site Setup (€250,000 transaction, permits, fencing, temporary utilities).
2. **Months 3–5:** Underground Infrastructure & Foundations (water wells, eco-sewage plant, solar grid, concrete foundations).
3. **Months 6–9:** Superstructure & Weatherproofing ("Red Shell" construction for 8 master houses, Holistic Center, Recovery Center).
4. **Months 10–14:** Utilities, Interior Finishes & Landscaping (HVAC, solar PV with battery storage, permaculture landscaping).
5. **Months 15–16:** Medical/Social Licensing, Staffing & Web3 Tokenization (DSP/ISU permits, medical staff recruitment, smart contract audit).
6. **Months 17–18:** Pilot Programs, Final Audit & Grand Opening (soft launch, formal inspection, full $NEXUS revenue-share activation).
